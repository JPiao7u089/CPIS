# CPIS
Conformal inference has attracted growing interest  for its ability to provide valid uncertainty quantification in predictions in a model-agnostic manner. In the context of inflammatory breast cancer (IBC), it is crucial to generate prediction intervals that achieve reliable coverage not only at the population level but also within clinically important subgroups to advance personalized medicine. However, standard conformal methods typically ensure only marginal coverage, which can be inadequate in heterogeneous settings where key subgroups exhibit distinct risk profiles.   Despite the clinical importance of subgroup-level prediction in survival analysis,  limited work has been conducted to provide  guarantees for subgroup coverage. To bridge  this gap, we integrate  weighted quantile regression with the  conformal inference framework to simultaneously control both marginal and subgroup-specific coverage probabilities in survival prediction. This approach produces more precise and informative prediction intervals for clinically relevant subgroups, while overcoming the limitations of small-sample subgroup estimation.  We establish asymptotic theoretical guarantees for subgroup-conditioned coverage and evaluate the finite-sample performance.  Applying our method to the National Cancer Database (NCDB) IBC cohort, we demonstrate improved prediction intervals with accurate overall and subgroup coverages,  supporting more informed clinical decision-making and personalized care.

The codes implements conformal inference methods for survival prediction, which ensure both marginal and subgroup-specific coverage guarantees. It integrates weighted quantile regression into the conformal inference framework to enable flexible, distribution-free prediction intervals that maintain valid coverage across user-defined subgroups.
